# Editorial rubric — what wins (and what does not)

Noise-to-Signal publishes **5 stake-oriented picks** each week for **venture-backed founders**, plus the noise÷signal ratio. FounderNexus is the room; this site is the public filter.

## What wins (include)

Picks that change a decision with skin in the game:

| Stake | Examples of signal |
| --- | --- |
| **Fundraising** | Terms, timing, who still gets to raise, comps that recalibrate a live process |
| **Hiring** | Org design after cuts, who to hire next, retention when capital is tight |
| **Board** | Governance, investor dynamics, hard conversations with people who write checks |
| **Runway** | Burn, pivots that shrink to survive, when to cut vs. double down |
| **GTM** | Buying committees, enterprise surfaces, pricing against the real market |

Every signal item must answer **why it matters** in operator language — not a press-release paraphrase.

## What does not win (exclude as primary)

- Mega-round cheerleading with no operator takeaway
- Invented or satirical “noise” framed as the week’s main insight
- Generic founder lifestyle / morning-routine / “build in public” theater
- Thread-bait frameworks with no stake attached
- Logo roundups that claim “the market is back” without cohort or terms context

Noise examples can lampoon the above; they are contrast, not the product.

## Weekly bar

1. Five picks, each mapped to at least one stake above.
2. Ratio + note that name the noise theme without celebrating volume.
3. UTMs on FounderNexus Apply CTAs use `utm_campaign=week-{weekOf}`.
4. Newsletter remains a stub until email is intentionally wired.

## Voice

Dry, specific, founder-facing. Prefer costs, tradeoffs, and filters over inspiration.
